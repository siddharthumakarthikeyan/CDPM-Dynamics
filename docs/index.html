Siddharth Umakarthikeyan

SASTRA Deemed to be University, India

Doctoral research · MATLAB / Simulink

CodeIsaac Sim versionBibTeX

Eight robots, one payload. Four quadrotors and four ground robots each hold one corner of a rigid platform; the cable tensions couple all nine bodies. Render of the same system in NVIDIA Isaac Sim.

Abstract

This model derives and simulates the combined dynamics of a quadrotor-based mobile cable-driven parallel manipulator: eight robots that share one payload. Four quadrotors (U1–U4) act as upper anchors and four two-wheel differential-drive mobile robots (L1–L4) act as lower anchors. Each robot is connected by a cable to one corner of a rigid moving platform, so every robot feels the platform and every other robot through the cable tensions.

The model is built in MATLAB/Simulink from individual robot dynamics upwards. Each cable produces a force only when it is taut, cable forces are transformed between body and global frames, and the platform responds to the sum of all eight cable forces and moments. The model is the dynamic foundation for the later control and simulation work on this system.

Model

  • Quadrotors (×4). Full 6-DoF rigid-body dynamics in each QuadRotor subsystem: translational and rotational accelerations from rotor thrust, gravity and the cable force at the attachment point, with body-to-global transformation.
  • Differential-drive mobile robots (×4). Planar dynamics driven by left and right wheel torques, wheel radius, track width and yaw inertia, plus the cable force at the winch point, transformed to the global frame.
  • Moving platform. Newton–Euler dynamics of a rigid box; each of its eight corners is located from the platform pose (position and roll, pitch, yaw) and receives one cable.
  • Cable model. For every cable, the force direction follows the vector between robot anchor and platform corner, and an isTension condition switches the force off when the cable would be in compression, so slack is represented.
  • Coupling. A force-generator stage collects the eight cable forces in x, y and z, applies them with opposite sign to each robot and to the platform, and closes the loop for the next integration step.
  • Visualisation. MATLAB scripts draw the body frame of all nine bodies (U1–U4, MP, L1–L4) over time from the logged poses.
